# about 

the project is for people who want to learn English in Arabic

## how does it work

1. select your goals, based on these goals:
  - the website brings the vocabularies you should learn to grow fast
  - spaced repeatetion to memorise these words

## vocabs

1. you see the word
2. input button to write the word
3. listen button that auto listens to the word
4. button "Next" to move to the next word
5. use **Spaced Repeatation** to repeat the words until u memorise them

## grammar

1. the platform embeds invidious videos to master the grammar
2. after each lesson u get grammar questions with results, each correct question = more points
3. grammar from A1 to C1, the user can skip what he doesn't want!

* pro feature: the user can link a video he wants and the platform auto shows us + **adding ai feature** to auto creates questions for this video based on it's idea (the user must explain the idea of the video or title etc..) + **video must be embedded from Invidious**

## listening

- Invidious videos for practice listening videos from A1-C1
- Written short stories, passages with AI listen button to reads it + questions for them

## writing

- essay/paragraph writing **Based on user GOALS!**
